# Demo Script

Five minutes, click by click. **Write this before building screens** — it tells you
exactly what must function and what can be beautiful cardboard.

Rehearse it five times on 31 July. Under pressure you will forget the order.

---

## Setup before walking in

- [ ] QR sticker for `MRV-8F3A21` printed and stuck on something portable
- [ ] Phone charged, browser open at `app.<domain>/scan`
- [ ] Laptop open at the dashboard
- [ ] Airplane-mode test done — know what breaks without wifi

---

## 0:00 — Open on the problem (no screen)

> Ask them: how do you currently know how many hours your ICU ventilators have run?

Let them answer. It's usually "we don't."

## 3:15 — Live scan (hand them the phone)

1. Scan the sticker
2. Gate evaluates → session starts
3. Timer runs
4. Tap **Equipment broke down**
5. Turn the laptop around — ticket created, unit down, alert fired

This is the moment. Don't rush it, don't narrate over it.
